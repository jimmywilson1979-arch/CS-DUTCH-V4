How it works

1. Blend. Att = a·xG + (1−a)·Scored, Def = a·xGA + (1−a)·Conceded, using the home column for the home side and the away column for the away side.

2. Shrink. Att* = (n·Att + K·L) / (n + K) with n = MP ÷ 2 (games at that venue). Home attack and away defence pull toward the league home average; away attack and home defence toward the away average. With K = 6, a team's own figures carry half the weight after 6 games at a venue, and more from there.

3. λ. λhome = Att*home × Def*away ÷ Lhome, λaway = Att*away × Def*home ÷ Laway — the same split-divisor step as the app's xG path.

4. Scores. Dixon-Coles grid with ρ, same maths as CS-DUTCH-V4.

The table shows xG only (a = 1, K = 0 — what the app does now), goals only and the blend, so you can see what the blend is moving. Finishing ratios far from 1.00 are where xG and reality disagree most.

a and K are starting values, not fitted ones. Log the blend λ against results and tune them later.
